DataFrame caching on serverless compute is now in Public Preview. You can cache a DataFrame with df.cache() or df.persist() on serverless compute to reuse an expensive result for the rest of your session. Caching requires serverless environment version 5 or above, Databricks Connect for Python 18.1.8 or above, or Databricks Connect for Scala 18.1.5 or above. The preview is enabled by default for most workspaces. Workspace admins can disable it from the Previews page. See Cache DataFrames on serverless compute.

October 23, 2026

The managed Google Analytics Aggregated connector is now available in Lakeflow Connect in Beta. Workspace admins can control access to this feature from the Previews page.

Use the connector to ingest aggregated Google Analytics 4 report data, such as user activity, acquisition, engagement, monetization, demographics, and tech reports, into Databricks. You can also define custom reports with your own dimensions, metrics, and aggregation level. The connector supports incremental ingestion and both service account and OAuth user-to-machine (U2M) authentication.

Files API 3.0 is in Beta and introduces two new capabilities:

  • Multipart uploads: Upload files larger than 5 GB to Unity Catalog volumes.

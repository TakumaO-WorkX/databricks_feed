Xero connector is available in Beta
> [翻訳エラー: Error code: 404 - {'type': 'error', 'error': {'type': 'not_found_error', 'message': 'model: claude-3-haiku-20240307'}, 'request_id': 'req_011CgYfGRCbNq817d6pKtSFH'}]
October 29, 2026

The managed Xero connector is now available in Beta in Databricks Lakeflow Connect. Workspace admins can enable it from the Previews page.

Use the connector to ingest accounting data such as invoices, payments, and contacts from Xero into Databricks. The connector uses OAuth user authorization, ingests transaction tables such as invoices and payments incrementally, and removes deleted records for supported tables.

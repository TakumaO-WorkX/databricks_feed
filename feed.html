Session restore for serverless jobs is now in Beta
> [翻訳エラー: Error code: 400 - {'type': 'error', 'error': {'type': 'invalid_request_error', 'message': 'Your credit balance is too low to access the Anthropic API. Please go to Plans & Billing to upgrade or purchase credits.'}, 'request_id': 'req_011CeBsWSWyxWWpxCJe6E6ZQ'}]
August 17, 2026

Restore the Python variables and Spark session from a serverless compute job run into a new interactive notebook, so you can debug a failure or explore results without rerunning the job. Databricks automatically captures a state snapshot when an eligible run fails, runs 30 minutes or longer, or is cancelled, and keeps it for 7 days after the run completes. This feature is in Beta. See Session restore for serverless jobs.

> [翻訳エラー: Error code: 400 - {'type': 'error', 'error': {'type': 'invalid_request_error', 'message': 'Your credit balance is too low to access the Anthropic API. Please go to Plans & Billing to upgrade or purchase credits.'}, 'request_id': 'req_011CeBsWUUJf1dyndZDbcChM'}]
August 17, 2026

Identity attributes are in Beta. You can now write Unity Catalog ABAC column mask policy conditions that target users by attributes synced from your identity provider, such as department or country, in addition to group membership. The has_identity_attribute_value and has_identity_attribute_tag_match functions are supported in the WHEN clause of column mask policies.

See identity attributes.
